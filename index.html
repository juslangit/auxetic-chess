Auxetic Chess

Chess where every 2×2 block turns a quarter after each move, carrying the pieces round with it — pawns too. A pawn still only ever moves forward, wherever the board puts it.

    A block cycles its contents a1→a2→b2→b1, — so a piece on a4 is carried to b4 — and a twist can never capture and nothing leaves its block.

    The board also folds, on d = a√2·cos(45°+θ): at 0° the tiles sit flush, at 45° the lattice is open and the area has doubled.
